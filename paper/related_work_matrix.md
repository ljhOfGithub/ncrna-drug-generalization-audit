## Nearest-neighbor conclusion

RAHGL-MDR already covers the broad framing “cold-start, leakage-safe, full-candidate ranking, strong degree baseline.” The remaining provisional contribution is narrower: one unified, reproducible audit that couples Bemis–Murcko scaffold separation, strong RNA-frequency priors, cross-database entity/pair overlap, temporal non-Mi drugs, and distinct-PMID replication targets, then shows how these choices reverse conclusions.

Thirty-two of the 35 cited works now have full-text or authoritative complete-source verification; the remaining qualified cases are limited to the already documented GCFMCL/SM2miR access boundary and final-versus-preprint version boundary for RNAsmol. The dimension-by-dimension direct-neighbor comparison remains in `verified_neighbor_comparison.md`. The gap remains **provisional** because a finite review cannot establish exhaustive absence. The paper must not use “first” or “novel.”
