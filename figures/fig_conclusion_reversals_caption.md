**Figure 1. Evaluation choices reverse conclusions in ncRNA–drug cold-start prediction.** Points show differences in full-candidate drug-macro average precision (candidate minus the stated baseline); bars show paired 95% bootstrap intervals, clustered by scaffold where applicable. The same residual estimator exceeds the adapted LocalGL baseline on Mi, but its gain over the RNA-frequency prior is small. Positive results on the raw ncRNADrug snapshot and the fixed RAHGL-MDR drug-cold folds disappear after entity de-overlap or scaffold-disjoint splitting. A model trained on independently re-supported pairs beats a prior defined on the same sparse target, yet falls below the stronger prior estimated from all reported pairs. Blue, gray, and orange denote intervals entirely above zero, crossing zero, and entirely below zero, respectively. These signs describe the stated comparison only and do not imply that a preregistered overall algorithm gate passed.
